01 · Why distribute a method of invention?

A conventional archive stores answers. A generative archive stores reusable descriptions of problems, constraints, components, procedures, evidence requirements and decisions. Independent people can assemble the pieces differently, test alternatives and retain their resulting work without the original author's involvement.

Generativity is bounded by truthful implementation: a prompt is not an application, a source compiler is not a deployed service, a simulation is not field evidence, and a self-recorded check is not external validation. This foundry offers a practical route from an idea to documented and portable artifacts, leaving real-world authority and judgment with people.

Its architecture synthesizes Genesis module assembly, Food Commons project protocols, the civic-resilience innovation genome, the 100-project meta-prompt portfolio, Occupation Atlas / Reward DNA pathways, Wizard Scroll learner autonomy, and evidence/provenance practices. These references represent design lineage discussed in this conversation, not proof of external adoption or original invention claims.

The user retains a right to reject the built-in framing, revise every generated prompt, choose a different intervention, preserve local copies or stop entirely. Gamification is restricted to documentation activity and may be ignored: there are no engagement streaks, artificial urgency or locked features.

02 · From problem to independent deployment

Observe: capture an actual baseline, population, constraints and credible alternative. Specify: select applicable methods and their stated failure modes. Generate: produce a blueprint or a bounded child tool and review every assumption. Authorize: secure permissions from people with real authority, including relevant Indigenous governments and rights-holders. Pilot: test safely on a reversible scale with published stop conditions. Measure: include negative and null results. Exchange: redact, review and share only what is consented. Improve: compare versions and keep a readable change record.

When AI is used outside this file, ask it to identify current sources with dates, uncertainty, contrary evidence, scope, licensing, security and implementation gaps. Require it to create tests rather than asserting that it ran them. For high-stakes domains, qualified external reviewers and field validation are necessary.

Recommended evidence packet: project identifier; hypothesis; baseline and denominator; intervention and comparator; governance permission; data collection method; risk register; explicit stop conditions; costs; outcomes with negative results; and a source-backed decision about whether to continue, change or discontinue.

03 · Scientific claims, causal inference and measurement

Separate observations, sourced claims, interpretations, hypotheses, simulations, metaphors and fictional content. Each claim should identify the relevant place, population, period, source, uncertainty and plausible alternatives. Do not infer transmission, causation or effectiveness from thematic coincidence.

Before/after change Δ = follow-up − baseline is descriptive. Its attribution to an intervention requires a suitable causal design, appropriate sampling and scrutiny of secular trends, selection, confounders, co-interventions, missing data and measurement reliability. Numeric outputs in this application are user-entered arithmetic, not validated forecasts.

For impact assessments, measure the outcome that matters: delivered and safe food, not merely planted trees; genuine learner competence, not XP; independently confirmed service delivery, not draft records; verified accessibility, not a checklist; and actual safe capability, not aspirational labels.

SHA-256 is a content-integrity primitive, not evidence of identity or historical precedence. An ordinary browser cannot recover arbitrary local absolute paths from a user-selected file; this app records the provided filename only. Browser time can be changed, and local ledgers can be edited. Cryptographic signatures and independently verified timestamp anchors are separate systems.

04 · Security, accessibility, offline limits and zero-harm

The standalone HTML has no external JavaScript, automatic uploads, accounts, telemetry, remote multiplayer, service worker or external model. The separately built hosted edition includes explicit same-origin offline caching. All selected files are processed locally with browser APIs. Portable JSON may still contain sensitive material; always inspect it before transferring. This file does not encrypt browser storage or authenticate other users.

File-scheme persistence is browser dependent. The app attempts IndexedDB, falls back to localStorage and finally volatile in-memory state. An explicit status reports the chosen mode. Backups are downloadable JSON, not invisible cross-device sync. Merging accepts only known schemas and preserves conflicting local record IDs; it does not reconcile simultaneous edits.

Use semantic HTML, native inputs, explicit labels, keyboard navigation, visible focus, contrast, reduced-motion support and plain-text equivalents. Manual testing is still necessary at small widths, 200% zoom, multiple browsers and with assistive technology. A checkmark is never a substitute for that testing.

Anti-inversion means design against the possibility that a beneficial tool becomes surveillance, coercion, exclusion, retaliation, private-data exposure or misinformation. Restrict permissions, keep sharing voluntary, avoid unverified claims and ensure exit, appeal and correction are possible. No software can guarantee zero harm.

A hosted installable PWA needs a real same-origin service worker and manifest served under HTTPS or localhost. A standalone file cannot self-register a blob or data URL service worker. This edition intentionally prioritizes portability over pretending that hosted features are already present.

Glossary & interface contracts

Blueprint

A versioned specification of what to build, what information it uses, and how it should be tested.

Knowledge pack

A portable, explicitly scoped package of non-executable records and metadata.

Falsifier

An observation or test result that would cause a claim or design to be revised or abandoned.

Regenerative outcome

A measured improvement in human or ecological conditions, not a project label or assumed intent.

Local-first

Primary functionality works on the local device; sharing and live online features are explicit, not inferred.

Independence

Participants can inspect, export, fork, refuse and exit without losing control of their own work.
